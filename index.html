HOW THE PONS LAUNCH ACTUALLY WORKS

The project does not receive the entire supply.

Designed to match onchain reality

Pons creates a fixed one-billion-token supply and launches the token with its WETH pool in one transaction. The pool liquidity is locked. BONES can only control the tokens obtained through the creator’s initial purchase and later creator-fee claims.

01

1B BONES minted

The fixed supply is created by the Pons launch contract and placed into the trading pool.

02

Creator buys up to 5%

The target is up to 50 million BONES. The exact result depends on the live quote, price impact and transaction execution.

03

Actual balances published

The launch transaction, token contract, pool and every project-controlled allocation transfer will be linked publicly.

No hidden “100% tokenomics”.

The 5% plan below is a target for the creator’s initial purchase—not a claim that the project owns or distributes the full supply. If less than 5% is obtained, community and founder amounts are reduced proportionally and the final figures replace the targets on this page.

WISHBONE TREASURY RULES

Purchases follow a published process.

01

Settlement window

At most once per week and only after a minimum public threshold is reached, so gas does not consume the purchase.

02

Smaller executions

Large amounts are divided into smaller transactions when liquidity or price impact requires it.

03

Public treasury

Purchased WISHBONE goes to the public treasury wallet and every transaction is added to the dashboard.

04

No price promise

Treasury purchases and burns do not guarantee an increase in BONES or WISHBONE value.

Treasury sale rule: WISHBONE will not be sold from the public treasury unless a new rule is published before any sale.

CLAIM-BASED AIRDROPS

Snapshots, exclusions and a cap per wallet.

Airdrops are not sent automatically to every address. Eligibility and allocations are calculated from a published snapshot, then claimed through a Merkle distributor. This reduces gas waste and prevents pools, contracts and project wallets from receiving rewards.
